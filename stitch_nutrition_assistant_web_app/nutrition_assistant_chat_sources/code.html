What are the food safety guidelines for storing leftover cooked lentils and quinoa, and do they provide complete proteins when paired?

You • 10:24 AM
eco

Nutrition Assistant

Scientific & Food Safety Synthesis
10:25 AM

Cooked lentils and quinoa should be cooled promptly and refrigerated in airtight, shallow glass containers within two hours of cooking at or below 40°F (4°C). They safely keep for 3 to 4 days, or up to 3 months frozen.

When paired throughout your day, quinoa (which is naturally a complete protein containing all nine essential amino acids) and lentils create a dense, highly bioavailable plant-protein profile rich in dietary fiber, iron, and folate without requiring heavy preparation.
